"Fancy harnesses make coding agents more expensive but not better, study finds" (The Decoder)

A controlled study shows that switching between coding-agent harnesses like Claude Code, OpenCode, or mini-SWE-agent barely moves pass rates more than rerunning the same setup. Cost per task, however, can triple, driven mainly by the system prompt that gets resent with every step.

My take: the headline matters less than what it signals about where the tooling is headed.
