"Nvidia bets at least $500 million on Inception's diffusion models to strengthen its open-source Nemotron lineup" (The Decoder)

Nvidia is close to acquiring AI startup Inception for at least $500 million, according to The Information. The company is just two years old and builds language models based on diffusion processes, an alternative approach to the token-by-token generation used by models like ChatGPT.
